2. What the app does

deskMonk runs on macOS, Windows and Linux. It scans the folders you choose and shows duplicates, junk files, old downloads and large files; you decide what is removed. Files are moved to the Trash, except files in the Junk category, which are deleted permanently. It also encrypts files and folders into a Vault, shows a disk map and your hardware, tests disk speed, runs a focus timer (Monk mode) and, in the version from our website, can put a password in front of apps you choose (App lock).
