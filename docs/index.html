What deskMonk does

Everything your computer needs, in one calm place

Instead of a cleaner, an encryption tool, a focus timer and a system profiler, you get one app that does all four, and does them without fuss.

Clean

Free up space without the fear of deleting the wrong file

Pick a folder or your whole home and press Scan. deskMonk sorts what it finds into four clear groups and shows how much space each one gives back, before anything is touched.

  • Duplicates: true copies only, matched byte for byte, so a file that merely has the same name is never flagged.
  • Junk files: app caches, the trash, temporary files, logs and old backups. Caches rebuild themselves when needed.
  • Old downloads: installers and files in Downloads you haven't touched for 90 days.
  • Large files: anything over 500 MB, the usual reason a disk is suddenly full.
  • You choose what goes. Everything except junk is moved to the Trash, so you can still change your mind.

Free: clean up to 2 GB a day. Pro: unlimited.

Vault

Lock away your private files with one password

Drop a file or a whole folder, choose a password, done. Your tax returns, passport scans and contracts become a single encrypted .dmvault file that nobody can open without the password.

  • Strong, modern encryption (ChaCha20-Poly1305 with an Argon2id password key), the kind password managers use.
  • All your vaults are listed in one place. Click Open, type the password, and your files are back.
  • Safe to keep in Dropbox, iCloud, Google Drive or on a USB stick: the cloud only ever sees scrambled data.
  • Opening your vaults is always free, on any plan, forever.

Free: 3 encryptions a day, up to 200 MB each. Pro: unlimited, any size.

Monk mode

Focus like a monk, rest like one too

A pomodoro timer with a friend. Press Start and the monk sits down to meditate while you work for 25 minutes; when it's time for a break, he stands up and stretches, and so should you.

  • Focus and break lengths you can set to fit how you work.
  • A gentle notification when a session ends, so you can stop watching the clock.
  • Counts your sessions, so you can see how much deep work you did today.

Free on every plan.

Disk map

See where your disk space went, at a glance

Every folder is drawn as a box sized by how much it holds. The biggest boxes are where your space went; click one to step inside, or open it in Finder or Explorer.

  • How full your disk is: used, total and free.
  • Finds the forgotten video project or the 40 GB of old phone backups in seconds.

Free on every plan.

Hardware

Know exactly what is inside your computer

Processor, memory, graphics, display, storage and battery, each with its brand and model, on one screen. Handy when you sell a laptop, buy a used one, or a support desk asks what you have.

  • Battery health and charge cycles: know when a battery is wearing out.
  • Disk speed test: real read and write speed of your drive in MB/s.
  • Memory maker, core counts, screen resolution and refresh rate, uptime.

Free on every plan.

App lock · macOS, Linux

Keep curious eyes out of your apps

Choose apps like Messages, Photos or WhatsApp. When someone opens one, deskMonk stops it and asks for your password; with the right password the app opens, and it locks again when it quits.

  • Ideal for a shared family Mac or a laptop you hand to someone for a minute.
  • One password for all locked apps.

Free: lock 1 app. Pro: lock as many as you like.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can deskMonk delete something important?

Nothing is deleted until you press Clean, and you see every file first. Everything except junk goes to the Trash, so you can put it back. Junk means caches, temporary files and logs that apps recreate on their own.

What if I forget a Vault password?

Then the vault can't be opened, not by you and not by us. That is what makes it safe. Write the password down somewhere safe.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. No trial period, no card, no account. The free plan has every feature with small daily limits; most people never need more.

How does Pro work on several computers?

After buying you get a license key. Enter it in deskMonk under "Have a license key?" on up to 3 computers. Bought from inside the app, that computer turns Pro by itself.

How do I cancel, or get a refund?

Cancel anytime from deskMonk (⭐ Pro → Manage subscription) or on your account page; you keep Pro until the end of the paid period. Within 14 days of paying you get a full refund, no questions asked.

Who handles the payment?
