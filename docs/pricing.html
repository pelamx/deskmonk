How buying works

  • The easiest way: in deskMonk, press ⭐ Get Pro. That computer turns Pro by itself after you pay.
  • Buying on this site gives you a license key right after payment. Enter it in deskMonk under "Have a license key?" on up to 3 computers.

  • Not happy? Ask within 14 days for a full refund. See the refund policy.
